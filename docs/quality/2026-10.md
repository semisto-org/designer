Three journeys block new features in their domain (paths in `journeys.yml`): sharing and invitations, billing, and the companion that greets a returning gardener. The editor itself holds up well: the person's own aerial photo, their plants with their adult crowns, the weather of the day, the date a plant went into the ground and its journal.

## Découvrir le site

- **Place ✗** — nothing on the way speaks about the visitor's own land: the time-lapse grows an example garden, and the end of the scroll leads to a table of regions and a footer, not to « Où est ton terrain ? ».
- **Time ✓** — the home page is the reference: thirty years go by as you scroll.
- **Companion ~** — the help center opens on the month's card (« Octobre, la saison de concevoir »), but the features page lists tools instead of showing when they serve.
- **Notebook ~** — the home page and the help center are the notebook; « Fonctionnalités » (a title and grids of cards) and « Tarifs » (plan cards, comparison table, FAQ) are the SaaS template the point of view refuses.
- **Further ✓** — « Ce mois-ci au jardin » in the help center is the kind of detail that anticipates.

Snags:
1. **Major** · end of the home scroll, both devices — the strongest moment ends on a list of regional data sources, with no field to type one's address (chantier 1, PR #254 to plug in).
2. **Major** · « Fonctionnalités » and « Tarifs », both devices — the SaaS template (chantier 3).
3. **Major** · home page, phone, during the scroll — the date label (« février 2049, an 23 du jardin-forêt ») sits under the chapter card (« octobre 2041 · an 15 — L'ombre a changé de place. »): two dates on top of each other, and the text is unreadable where they cross.
4. **Minor** · « Se connecter », both devices — a plain form; nothing reminds the visitor of the garden they just saw grow.

## Créer sa première carte

- **Place ~** — the address comes second, after a name; then the carnet de route opens on an example terrain (« Terrain d'exemple : le tien est derrière ce carnet ») right after the person gave theirs.
- **Time ✓** — the welcome page draws « an 1, an 5, an 15… ton jardin grandit ici », and the carnet de route explains why the order matters over thirty years.
- **Companion ✓** — the four steps on « Mes cartes », the carnet de route, « Délimitons maintenant ton terrain ».
- **Notebook ~** — the welcome page is a notebook spread; « Nouvelle carte » and the empty « Mes cartes » are ordinary forms and cards.
- **Further ~** — the region is recognised as soon as the address is chosen (« Données de la région : Wallonie »).

Snags:
1. **Major** · « Le terrain ouvert », both devices — the carnet de route covers the person's own place with an example terrain at the very moment they first see it.
2. **Major** · « Nouvelle carte », both devices — the map's name is asked before the place, and the preview opens on all of Western Europe (chantier 1).
3. **Minor** · « Lien demandé », both devices — the confirmation that the link was sent only shows as a toast on the next page.
4. **Minor** · « Je conçois mon lieu », both devices — the question « particulier ou pro » stands between the link and the person's land.

## Inviter quelqu’un

- **Place ~** — the owner shares from their map, but the invited person first lands on a generic sign-in page (the map's name only in a toast), then on the carnet de route's example terrain.
- **Time ✗** — nothing in the invitation, on either side, says this is a garden that will grow, or since when it is being designed.
- **Companion ~** — the dialog explains « Propriétaires du lieu » at length, but says nothing of what the invited person will be able to do, nor what to do once they arrive.
- **Notebook ✗** — a standard sharing dialog, and the invited person's first screen is the generic sign-in form.
- **Further ~** — « Bienvenue sur la carte « Le jardin de Purnode » » greets the invited person by the map's name.

Snags:
1. **Major** · « Partager », both devices — inviting by e-mail, the reason the dialog was opened, comes after the long « Le lieu et ses plantes » block; on the phone it is below the fold.
2. **Major** · « Le lien reçu », both devices — the invited person sees no map, no place and no name of who invited them, only a sign-in form and a toast.
3. **Major** · « Invitée sur la carte », both devices — the carnet de route shows the example terrain instead of the garden they were invited to.
4. **Minor** · « Invitation envoyée », phone — the toast covers the e-mail field it confirms.

## Payer

- **Place ✗** — « Formule et facturation » never names the person's map; « Cartes actives : 1 sur 1 » is a counter, not « Le jardin de Purnode ».
- **Time ✗** — nothing says what a year of Home does for a garden, or that nothing is deleted when it ends.
- **Companion ~** — the free plan's card explains what Home adds, but the limit itself arrives as an error.
- **Notebook ✗** — four plan cards side by side, the pricing grid of any SaaS (chantier 4).
- **Further ~** — the return from Stripe says the page updates itself, and Semisto members are told about their price.

Snags:
1. **Major** · « La limite de la formule », both devices — at the free plan's limit, the « Nouvelle carte » page (opened by its address; « Mes cartes » hides its button) still shows the whole form; after the person has typed it, the map is refused with a red error toast (« Ta formule ne permet pas de nouvelle carte… ») and what they typed is lost.
2. **Major** · « Mes cartes », both devices — at the limit, « Nouvelle carte » disappears and only « Passer au forfait » remains, with no word of why.
3. **Major** · « Formules », both devices — the plans page opens on a grid of four cards rather than on what the plan does for the person's places (chantier 4).
4. **Minor** · « Retour après paiement », both devices — the thanks sit above « Formule gratuite » until the webhook lands; the two contradict each other for a moment.

## Planter au jardin

The phone app was not replayed; this journey ran in the web editor on a phone.

- **Place ✓** — the person's plants on their own aerial photo, numbered.
- **Time ✓** — « Planté le 19 octobre 2026 », the plant's journal (« sa reprise, sa floraison… »), the adult crown (11,5 m) on the sheet.
- **Companion ~** — « À faire : Remplir la fiche projet » and the « Observer » step stay in the header while the person is planting.
- **Notebook ~** — the plant's sheet is a form: name, notes and labels come before anything about planting.
- **Further ~** — after planting, the journal invites the next note; but nothing on the phone puts « Marquer comme planté » within reach.

Snags:
1. **Major** · « Un plant », phone — « Marquer comme planté », the gesture of the day in the garden, is far below name, notes, labels, number, conduct and grafting; the person must scroll the sheet to find it.
2. **Major** · all steps, both devices — the journey's header and « À faire maintenant » still say « Observer ton terrain · Remplir la fiche projet » while plants go into the ground.
3. **Minor** · « Un plant », phone — the sheet covers the map, so the plant being marked is hidden.

## Revenir après trois mois

- **Place ✓** — « Mes cartes » shows the garden's sketch « aujourd'hui » and the editor opens on it.
- **Time ~** — « Là où tu t'étais arrêté·e · il y a 3 mois » and the season's line on « Mes cartes »; in the editor, nothing says the twelve trees have been in the ground for three months.
- **Companion ✗** — after three months, the next step offered is still « Remplir la fiche projet »; nothing about what the season asks of a young planting (checking it took, mulching, watering).
- **Notebook ~** — « Mes cartes » is a notebook page; the editor is the editor.
- **Further ✗** — no « since your last visit », no reminder of what was planted when, no task for the season.

Snags:
1. **Major** · « Mes cartes », both devices — « Palette vide : choisis les espèces qui te font envie » next to a garden of twelve planted trees.
2. **Major** · « Le jardin trois mois plus tard », both devices — no welcome back: the same « À faire maintenant » as on the first day.
3. **Major** · « Ce qui a poussé », both devices — the planting list says « 12 plantés » but not when, nor how old each tree is now.
